## Overview

**UniLearn** is an AI-based smart learning platform designed to support students with different accessibility and learning needs.

The platform adapts the learning experience based on individual requirements, helping students with **visual, hearing, speech, and learning disabilities** access educational content in a more comfortable and effective way.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Most digital learning platforms follow a **one-size-fits-all approach**, which can create accessibility barriers for students with disabilities.

Students may struggle with:

* Accessing educational content in suitable formats
* Understanding complex learning material
* Communicating through conventional interfaces
* Maintaining consistent learning progress

At the same time, creating multiple accessible versions of educational content can be difficult and time-consuming for educators.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

UniLearn brings **accessibility, AI assistance, and personalized learning** together in one platform.

Students can select an accessibility mode based on their needs, while the platform adapts content, interaction, and learning assistance accordingly.

### Accessibility Modes

**Blind Mode**

* Text-to-speech support
* Audio-based navigation
* Clear focus indicators

**Dyslexia Mode**

* Improved typography and spacing
* Simplified explanations
* Readability-focused interface

**Hearing Disability Mode**

* Persistent captions
* Visual alerts
* Text-based tutoring

**Speech Disability Mode**

* Text-based communication
* Voice-input support
* Alternative interaction methods

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### AI Voice Assistant — Uni

An interactive voice assistant that uses speech recognition and speech synthesis to answer student queries and provide learning assistance based on the active accessibility mode.

### Interactive Quizzes

Subject-wise quizzes with instant scoring, answer explanations, and performance feedback.

### Progress Dashboard

Tracks study hours, quiz scores, learning streaks, badges, and subject-wise performance.

### Daily Challenges

Short daily activities designed to encourage consistent learning and student engagement.

### Learner Insights

Provides personalized insights into strengths, improvement areas, learning patterns, and recommended focus areas.

### Accessibility Controls

Includes high-contrast mode, adjustable font sizes, and large-text support across the platform.

It explores integration with AI services for:

* Conversational tutoring
* Speech-to-text
* Text-to-speech
* AI-based student assistance
* Offline fallback support

## 🌟 Impact

UniLearn aims to make digital education **more accessible, personalized, and inclusive** by adapting the learning environment to the needs of individual students.

Rather than expecting every student to learn in the same way, UniLearn focuses on creating a learning experience that **adapts to the learner**.
